# Disaster Rescue

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Residents report and track incidents on the website and Flutter application. Dispatchers triage reports and assign teams; responders acknowledge assignments and record progress; disaster management officers monitor incidents and export summaries. Administrators manage access, teams and settings.

## Product Purpose

Coordinate location-based disaster rescue reports from submission through verification, assignment, response, and closure. Preserve the existing workflows and role permissions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing React/TypeScript web app, Flutter application, Express API, Firebase authentication and Firestore. API is the authority for mutations. Airen authorized Codex on October 17 to implement the redesign across every web route and Flutter. Preserve existing working-tree repairs and backend policy. Test against isolated local emulators. No production deployment is requested.

## Brand Commitments

Airen requested a complete visual redesign and explicitly delegated direction selection on October 16, 2026. The previous navy/blue identity is not binding. Existing product names and factual safety information remain. No verified public hotline or resident-app download exists in the repository.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md, web/src, docs, and design-plans contain product behavior and prior browser evidence. Synthetic emulator fixtures are demonstrations, not live incidents. Course credits must remain factual.

## Product Principles

- Make urgency, location, status, and next action easy to distinguish.
- Keep actions consistent with the signed-in user's permissions.
- Keep emergency guidance honest and separate from software availability.
- Verify responsive layouts with realistic and empty data.

## Open Decisions

Operational ambient lighting and physical device usage have not been confirmed. A readable light workspace is the design hypothesis, not a researched user finding. Real hotline, privacy review, and public app distribution remain unresolved.
