## Fixes applied (test first)

1. Skip links and `#main-content` targets added to sign-in, registration, and Forbidden. (Test: `web/src/pages/AccessPageLandmarks.test.tsx`.)
2. Page titles set on sign-in, registration, and Forbidden. The sign-in page showed a stale title after an in-app sign-out.
3. Resident layout: the second `<header>` (`resident-band`) became a `<div>`. It exposed a duplicate top-level banner landmark. CSS uses class selectors only, so no styling changed. (Test: `ResidentLayout.test.tsx`.)
4. Favicon: `web/public/favicon.svg` added and linked from `web/index.html`. Verified: `/favicon.svg` returns 200 `image/svg+xml`.

After fixes, verified in the browser: titles, skip targets, favicon, and one banner on `/my`.

## Not verified

- Loading, long-content, and empty states across staff routes beyond the route-level checks.
- Mutations: no report was submitted and no staff action was taken in this pass.
- Flutter on a device or simulator: no device or emulator was run.
- Phone widths were measured in a 390px iframe. The browser window itself cannot go below 500px here.
