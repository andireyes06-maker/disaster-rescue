- **C1, late provider completion (fixed):** `not_found` no longer makes a row terminal. Rows move to `reconciling` and are re-checked with capped backoff (first re-check 15 minutes, doubling to 24 hours). Only a confirmed destroy deletes a row. Committed assets are never destroyed. Process-death test: a provider upload that completes after deadline plus grace, with no request continuation, is removed by a later drain. Tests: API 421 passing; emulator adapters 19/19 (including a real-emulator lease of a reconciling row). Not verified: real Cloudinary, a real crash mid-upload.
- **C1, unkeyed uploads (fixed by internal identity):** a server-generated attempt key (random, never sent to or accepted from the client) routes unkeyed requests through the same reservation, fencing and reconciliation as keyed ones. It does NOT deduplicate client retries; each unkeyed request is its own attempt. Each such request creates one submission claim row with no TTL (disclosed, same as keyed claims). The alternative (reject photo requests without a key) was not chosen; it needs approval.
